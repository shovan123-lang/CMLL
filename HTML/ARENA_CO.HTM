Match observations: 

THE DINAMITAS SILENCE GUADALAJARA
Lucha Libre en Domingo | Guadalajara, Arena Coliseo Guadalajara | 1991-07-14

Rayo de Jalisco Jr. came home to Guadalajara on Sunday night and gave the
Arena Coliseo the first fall it wanted. The rest of the night belonged to
Los Hermanos Dinamita.

Rayo, Atlantis and Konnan, the National Heavyweight Champion, faced Cien
Caras, Máscara Año 2000 and Universo 2000 over two out of three falls. At
12:39 Rayo locked Máscara Año 2000 in the Cavernaria, and the building came
apart. Then the Dinamitas went to work on Konnan. Cien Caras slammed the
champion and pinned him to even the match, and after more than thirty
minutes Universo 2000 came off the top with a flying bodypress and pinned
Konnan again for the deciding fall.

Cien Caras stood over Konnan with the belt held up in the air, the same
Cien Caras who protested when Vampiro got the title shot he wanted. Konnan
defends that title against Vampiro's hair at Verano de Escándalo on July
26. He meets Caras in Acapulco on Wednesday.

Blue Panther's night was short and ugly. He had Sangre Chicana in trouble
when he lost his head, refused the referee's warnings and was disqualified
at 4:37. Afterward he grabbed the microphone, and it wasn't Chicana he
wanted. It was El Dandy, the Light Heavyweight Champion who ran in on him
in Mexico City the night before. Panther said he'll find Dandy anywhere
they put him.

Toshiyo Yamada beat Lola González in a lightning match with the reverse
Gory special bomb at 10:05, and the two women shook hands afterward to a
standing ovation. Apolo Dantes rolled up Pierroth Jr. at 12:36, and Felino
and Silver King beat Tony Arce and Vulcano in two straight falls, Felino
taking both.
