Match observations: 

Coliseo Acapulco
Acapulco, Guerrero
July 17, 1991
Attendance: 2,500
Average ticket: $10.80
Gate: $27,000

CIEN CARAS COUNTS OUT THE CHAMPION

CMLL came to the Coliseo Acapulco for the first time on Wednesday night,
and the coast got a main event nobody expected. Cien Caras beat Konnan,
the Mexican National Heavyweight Champion, by countout at 15:54. The title
doesn't change hands on a countout, so Konnan keeps the belt. He does not
keep much else. Three nights ago in Guadalajara Caras pinned him in the
trios. Tonight he left him on the floor while the referee counted to
twenty. Nine days before Konnan puts his title up against Vampiro's hair
at Verano de Escandalo, the biggest Dinamita has made his point: the
champion has a problem who isn't Vampiro.

A LADDER IN ACAPULCO

The match the people will talk about was the ladder match between
Atlantis and the American Irwin R. Schyster. For fifteen minutes they
fought up and down the ladder, and neither man could take it home. The
bell rang on a draw at the time limit, and the crowd stood and applauded
both of them. Schyster came to Mexico as a guest. After tonight he may
have a reason to come back.

LIGER STEALS THE SHOW

Jushin Liger and The Blue Blazer, the masked visitors from Japan and
North America, beat Ultimo Dragon and The Lightning Kid in a fast, wild
match. Liger finished it with the moonsault on Dragon at 13:36. Dragon
has spent the summer learning the Mexican style. Liger showed him what
the Japanese juniors will be waiting for.

THE BROTHERS ROLL ON

Negro Casas and Felino are still unbeaten as a team. Their match with Art
Barr and Jerry Estrada didn't last long. Felino and Barr spilled to the
floor, Barr didn't make it back in, and the referee counted him out at
5:06. Barr argued all the way to the back.

AROUND THE RING

La Diabolica pinned Lola Gonzalez with a flying headscissors at 13:30,
her second win over Lola this month. Vulcano picked up his first CMLL win
when he flattened Tony Arce with the flying splash at 10:24.
