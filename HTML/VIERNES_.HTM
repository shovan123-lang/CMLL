Match observations: 

Arena Mexico
Mexico City
July 19, 1991
Attendance: 15,500
Average ticket: $13.50
Gate: $209,250

KONNAN STOPS THE SLIDE

Konnan walked into Arena Mexico on Friday night with three straight
losses behind him, and he left them in the ring. The Mexican National
Heavyweight Champion teamed with Blue Panther and Negro Casas against
Vampiro Canadiense, Kamala and Black Magic. At 15:13 he caught Black
Magic with a victory roll and held him down for three. One week from
tonight Konnan puts his title up against Vampiro's hair at Verano de
Escandalo. For the first time in a week, he goes into it a winner.

THE GUERREROS ARE IN THE FINAL

Eddie Guerrero, Chavo Guerrero and Fuerza Guerrera beat Atlantis, Angel
Azteca and Rayo de Jalisco Jr. in two straight falls, and Chavo took
both of them. He came off the top with the moonsault on Azteca at 15:32,
then did it again to Rayo at 32:19. It was the best match of the night.
Fuerza never got near a fall. He celebrated with the family in the ring,
but he didn't look like a man who liked how they got there.

SANTO, VOLADOR AND DRAGON ADVANCE

The first semifinal went almost forty minutes. El Hijo del Santo,
Volador and Ultimo Dragon beat Los Brazos in two straight falls, and
Dragon, who had lost his last two matches, ended it with a brainbuster
on Brazo de Oro at 39:53. The Brazos gave them everything they had. Next
Friday at Verano de Escandalo, Santo, Volador and Dragon meet the
Guerreros for the first CMLL World Trios Championship.

LA DIABOLICA HAS A PARTNER

Pantera Surena made her CMLL debut in a lightning match against Lola
Gonzalez and won it at 8:29, rolling Lola up with her feet on the ropes.
The referee never saw the feet. Lola is still looking for her first win
of the month, and tomorrow night at Arena Coliseo she and Toshiyo Yamada
face La Diabolica and Pantera together.

MASAKRE CAN'T KEEP HIS HANDS OFF

Octagon, The Lightning Kid and Vulcano beat Masakre, Pierroth Jr. and
the American Rob Zakowski in two straight falls. It ended at 26:27, when
Masakre went after Octagon's mask and the referee threw him out of the
match. Masakre has watched Octagon for a week. Tonight he couldn't wait
any longer.

A FIRST LOSS FOR FELINO AND SILVER KING

Sangre Chicana and Apolo Dantes beat Silver King and Felino in two
straight falls. Chicana took the first when Felino couldn't beat the
count back in at 13:52, and Dantes finished Silver King with a flying
splash at 20:20. It's the first loss for Felino and Silver King as a
team.

NEXT: Arena Coliseo, Saturday, July 20. Toshiyo Yamada and Lola Gonzalez
against La Diabolica and Pantera Surena.
