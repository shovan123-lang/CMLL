Match observations: 

CHAVO TAKES THE PIN
Sábado de Lucha Libre | Mexico City, Arena Coliseo | 1991-07-13

The Torneo de Tercias filled out its semifinals at Arena Coliseo on
Saturday night, and the Guerrero family is through. Whether the family is
still together is another question.

Eddie, Chavo and Fuerza Guerrera went more than twenty-one minutes with
Kamala, Black Magic and Art Barr in the main event. Late in the match
Fuerza had Black Magic set up and went for the cover. Chavo shoved him
aside, hooked Black Magic for the wheelbarrow stunner and took the pin
himself. Fuerza stared at him all the way to the back. Eddie walked between
them.

Los Brazos are the other semifinalists from Saturday. Brazo de Oro, Brazo
de Plata and El Brazo beat Cien Caras, Máscara Año 2000 and Universo 2000
when Plata flattened Universo with the Porky Plancha. Cien Caras didn't
blame his brothers. He blamed Vampiro, who has had Caras's attention since
he took the title shot Caras wanted.

That sets the semifinals for Arena México on Friday: El Hijo del Santo,
Volador and Ultimo Dragón against Los Brazos, and Atlantis, Rayo de Jalisco
Jr. and Ángel Azteca against the Guerreros.

Octagón had his first singles match in CMLL and made Espectro Jr. submit to
the Octaclutch at 11:51 of a lightning match. The crowd was standing for
the last three minutes. Masakre watched the whole thing from the entrance
and didn't come any closer.

Volador and El Hijo del Santo kept the Mexican National Tag Team
Championship against Jerry Estrada and Javier Cruz. Santo took the deciding
fall when he bent Cruz back in the camel clutch at 13:38.

Satánico and MS-1 beat Blue Panther and Ultimo Dragón when Satánico caught
Panther with a rolling fallaway slam. El Dandy, the third Infernal, ran in
during the match and went straight for Panther. It took referees and half
the locker room to pull them apart.
